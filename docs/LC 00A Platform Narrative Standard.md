PURPOSE
This document defines the narrative, emotional and participation framework that governs LegacyCapsule.
It exists to ensure that every touchpoint across the platform communicates a consistent story, purpose and experience.
This document influences:
	Homepage
	Tribute Room
	Profile Page
	Legacy Room
	Event Story
	Keepsake Cards
	Share Messages
	Emails
	Publications
	QR Participation Materials

THE FUNDAMENTAL BELIEF
Important stories are lost every day.
Photographs become scattered.
Messages disappear.
Memories remain untold.
Voices are forgotten.
LegacyCapsule exists to prevent that loss.

PLATFORM DEFINITION
LegacyCapsule is a collaborative story preservation platform that helps families, friends and communities capture, preserve and publish the complete story of life’s most meaningful people and events.
LegacyCapsule is not a tribute wall.
LegacyCapsule is not an event website.
LegacyCapsule is not a photo gallery.
LegacyCapsule brings together every voice, memory, photograph and milestone to create a permanent record that can be shared, revisited and preserved.

THE CORE MISSION
Capture.
Preserve.
Publish.
These three actions define the platform.

Capture
Gather voices, memories, tributes, photographs and experiences from everyone who matters.

Preserve
Organise contributions into a meaningful and lasting record.

Publish
Create a professionally assembled publication that tells the complete story.

THE STORY PRINCIPLE
Every meaningful person.
Every meaningful event.
Creates a story.
The platform exists to help tell that story completely.
The more voices included, the richer the story becomes.

THE PARTICIPATION PRINCIPLE
People do not participate because they are asked.
People participate because they believe their contribution matters.
Therefore:
Every interaction should reinforce:
	You belong here.
	Your contribution matters.
	Your voice is unique.
	The story is richer because of your participation.

THE COMPLETENESS PRINCIPLE
Every story is incomplete without its people.
The platform should encourage participation through inclusion rather than promotion.
Preferred framing:
“Who else should be part of this story?”
Avoid framing:
“Please share this link.”
The focus is preserving missing voices, not driving traffic.

CONTRIBUTOR PHILOSOPHY
Contributors are not content creators.
Contributors are people helping preserve a story.
The platform should minimise internal terminology.
Users should rarely need to learn LegacyCapsule-specific language.
Use familiar language wherever possible.
Examples:
	Contributor
	Family
	Friend
	Guest
	Participant
Avoid unnecessary platform jargon.

PUBLICATION PHILOSOPHY
The publication is not an afterthought.
The publication is the destination.
Every contribution ultimately exists to help create the complete record.
Tributes.
Photos.
Videos.
Event-Day Moments.
Participation Metrics.
Event Chapters.
All contribute to the final publication.

THE BELONGING PRINCIPLE
Visitors should immediately understand:
You have been invited because you matter to this story.
The platform should create belonging before requesting participation.
Every major entry point should answer:
Why am I here?
Before answering:
What should I do?

THE HOMEPAGE NARRATIVE
Homepage messaging should follow:
Frame → Emotional Promise → Proof → Participation Path
The homepage must explain:
What LegacyCapsule is
Before explaining:
What LegacyCapsule does

THE TRIBUTE ROOM NARRATIVE
The Tribute Room is the participation gateway.
Visitors arriving directly from shared links should receive orientation before contribution prompts.
The Tribute Room should establish:
	Belonging
	Purpose
	Community Presence
	Contribution Value
Before displaying contribution actions.

THE LEGACY ROOM NARRATIVE
The Legacy Room exists to show the story growing.
It is not an analytics dashboard.
It demonstrates:
	Community participation
	Story growth
	Collective contribution
	Preservation progress
The emotional response should be:
“Look how many people helped build this.”
Not:
“Look at the numbers.”

THE EVENT STORY NARRATIVE
Events are not collections of photos.
Events are collections of moments.
Each event phase represents a chapter in a larger story.
Examples:
Wedding ├── Traditional Wedding ├── White Wedding └── Reception
Memorial ├── Wake Keep ├── Funeral Service ├── Interment └── Thanksgiving
The Event Story module exists to preserve those chapters.

THE SHARE PHILOSOPHY
Sharing should be framed as inclusion.
Not promotion.
Preferred:
“Who else should be part of this story?”
Avoid:
“Share this link.”
Sharing should help bring missing voices into the record.

THE QR PARTICIPATION PHILOSOPHY
QR codes are participation gateways.
They should invite guests to help preserve the event.
Examples:
Help Write This Chapter
Add Your Moment To The Record
Help Preserve Today’s Story
The emphasis is contribution and preservation.
Not file uploads.

THE EMAIL PHILOSOPHY
Every email should reinforce:
Belonging.
Contribution.
Permanence.
Community.
The contributor should consistently feel:
My contribution mattered.

THE PARTICIPATION LOOP
Every successful participant journey follows:
Welcome
↓
Belonging
↓
Contribution
↓
Recognition
↓
Return Visit
↓
Sharing
↓
Publication
↓
Anniversary Reflection

THE LONG-TERM PROMISE
A contributor should be able to return years later and still find:
Their words.
Their photographs.
Their contribution.
Their place in the story.

FOUNDER PRINCIPLE
People may forget the platform.
They should never forget the story they helped preserve.
Success is not measured by submissions.
Success is measured by how completely a story was captured, preserved and passed on.
